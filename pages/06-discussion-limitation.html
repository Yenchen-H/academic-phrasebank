DIS

討論與限制

Discussion & Limitation

解釋結果的意義、與前人比較，並誠實交代研究限制與未來方向。

寫作提醒

  • 討論的順序可以是：主要發現 → 與文獻比較 → 可能原因 → 實務意涵 → 限制與未來研究。
  • 與前人結果不同時，提出可能原因，不要只說 different。
  • 限制寫得具體反而加分，並搭配一句未來研究可以怎麼改善。
DIS-01

總結主要發現

This study found that [[main finding]], which suggests that [[meaning]].

This study found that decision making was the most influential indicator, which suggests that cognitive ability deserves more attention in selection.

討論第一段先重述最重要的發現，再用 which suggests 帶出意義。

DIS-02

說明與前人結果一致

This finding is consistent with [[previous study]], which reported that [[result]].

This finding is consistent with earlier work on youth basketball, which reported that tactical awareness predicted later performance.

與文獻一致時使用，增加結果可信度。

DIS-03

說明與前人結果不同並推測原因

In contrast to [[previous study]], our results showed [[finding]]. One possible reason is that [[explanation]].

In contrast to studies on adult players, our results showed that physical indicators were less important. One possible reason is that youth players are still in different stages of maturation.

One possible reason is that 保守又清楚，比 This is because 更適合推測性的解釋。

DIS-04

說明實務意涵

These results imply that [[stakeholders]] should [[recommendation]].

These results imply that coaches should include cognitive tests in the early stage of selection.

把研究發現轉成具體建議，回應 Introduction 提到的實務需求。

DIS-05

坦承研究限制

This study has some limitations. First, [[limitation]], so the results may not generalize to [[other context]].

This study has some limitations. First, the sample was drawn from a single region, so the results may not generalize to other countries.

限制要說明「會造成什麼影響」，而不只是列出缺點。

DIS-06

提出未來研究方向

Future research could [[direction]] to [[purpose]].

Future research could include longitudinal data to examine whether the selected players perform better over time.

通常緊接在限制之後，一個限制對應一個未來方向，讀起來最順。
