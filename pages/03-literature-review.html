LIT

文獻探討

Literature Review

依主題整理前人研究並加以評析，最後導向本研究要補的缺口。

寫作提醒

  • 依「主題或方法」分段，不要一篇接一篇寫成流水帳。
  • 每段結尾要有一句自己的評論（這些研究做到什麼、還缺什麼），只列研究不評論是審稿人常挑的問題。
  • 最後一段要做總結，並把讀者帶到你的研究缺口。
  • 時態：研究趨勢用現在完成式，描述特定研究的做法與發現用過去式。

段落結構

  1. 定義核心概念LIT-01
  2. 依主題回顧研究LIT-02LIT-03LIT-04
  3. 比較與評析LIT-05LIT-06
  4. 小結並銜接缺口LIT-07LIT-08
LIT-01

定義核心概念

[[Concept]] refers to [[definition]].

Talent identification refers to the process of recognizing players who have the potential to perform at a higher level.

文獻探討開頭先統一名詞定義，避免審稿人對概念理解不同。

LIT-02

介紹單篇研究的做法與發現

[[A previous study]] applied [[method]] to [[purpose]] and found that [[finding]].

A previous study applied the fuzzy AHP to rank coaching criteria and found that tactical knowledge received the highest weight.

正式寫作時把 A previous study 換成 Author et al. (year)。一句同時交代方法與發現，資訊密度剛好。

LIT-03

歸納多篇研究的一致發現

Several studies have reported that [[finding]] [[citations]].

Several studies have reported that relative age affects selection decisions in youth football [12, 15, 18].

多篇研究結論相同時合併成一句，引用集中放在句尾，比逐篇介紹精簡。

LIT-04

說明研究之間的主要差異

Studies in this area differ mainly in [[aspect]].

Studies in this area differ mainly in how expert opinions are collected and aggregated.

用來開啟一段比較，接下來分別說明不同做法，讀者會知道你在整理而不是列清單。

LIT-05

比較兩種方法的優缺點

Compared with [[method A]], [[method B]] is better at [[strength]] but requires [[cost]].

Compared with crisp AHP, fuzzy AHP is better at handling vague expert judgments but requires more computation.

優點與代價放在同一句，語氣平衡，適合用來說明為什麼選擇某個方法。

LIT-06

評析既有研究的不足

These studies provide useful insight into [[topic]], but they mostly [[limitation]].

These studies provide useful insight into indicator selection, but they mostly rely on small expert panels.

放在每段回顧的最後，先肯定再指出不足，這就是文獻探討中的「評論」。

LIT-07

總結文獻並銜接本研究

Taken together, the literature shows that [[summary]]. This study therefore focuses on [[focus]].

Taken together, the literature shows that expert-based and data-driven approaches have rarely been combined. This study therefore focuses on integrating the two.

文獻探討的最後一段，用 Taken together 收束，再用 therefore 接到自己的研究。

LIT-08

由文獻歸納研究需求

The reviewed literature indicates a clear need for research on [[topic]] in [[context]].

The reviewed literature indicates a clear need for research on interpretable selection models in youth team sports.

回顧完多個面向的研究後用這句收尾，直接把文獻導向研究需求。原文用 substantive need，改成 clear need 比較自然。
