GAP

研究缺口

Research Gap

清楚指出既有研究還沒解決的問題，這是審稿人判斷論文價值的關鍵。

寫作提醒

  • 研究缺口要獨立成一句、甚至一小段，不要藏在長段落中間。
  • 先肯定前人做了什麼，再說還缺什麼，語氣保守比較不會被挑戰。
  • 避免寫 no study has ever...，除非你非常確定，改用 few studies 或 remains limited。
  • 常見的缺口類型：沒人做過、方法有限制、結果不一致、缺乏驗證、評估方式不實用。

段落結構

  1. 讓步，肯定前人成果GAP-01
  2. 指出具體缺口GAP-02GAP-03GAP-04GAP-05GAP-07GAP-08
  3. 總結需求，銜接研究目的GAP-06
GAP-01

指出既有研究忽略的面向

Although [[previous studies]] have examined [[A]], few have considered [[B]].

Although previous studies have examined physical fitness indicators, few have considered how coaches weigh them in practice.

先讓步再轉折，few have 比 no study has 保守，是最安全的缺口句型。

GAP-02

說明某議題仍缺乏研究

However, [[topic]] remains largely unexplored, especially in [[context]].

However, the interpretability of selection models remains largely unexplored, especially in youth football.

remains largely unexplored 很常見，especially in 把缺口縮到你的研究範圍，避免講得太大。

GAP-03

指出既有方法的限制

Most existing approaches rely on [[assumption or resource]], which limits their use in [[setting]].

Most existing approaches rely on marker-based motion capture, which limits their use in daily training sessions.

用 which limits 直接接後果，讓讀者知道這個限制為什麼重要。

GAP-04

指出兩類研究之間沒有整合

To date, [[approach A]] and [[approach B]] have mostly been studied separately.

To date, expert judgment and machine learning prediction have mostly been studied separately.

適合「結合兩種方法」型的研究，下一句通常就接 This study combines...。

GAP-05

說明現有結果不一致

Findings on [[topic]] are inconsistent, and the reasons for these differences are not yet clear.

Findings on the relative importance of technical and physical indicators are inconsistent, and the reasons for these differences are not yet clear.

文獻結論互相矛盾時使用，可以合理化你為什麼要再做一次。

GAP-06

用一句話總結研究缺口

Therefore, there is a need for [[a method or study]] that can [[goal]] while [[condition]].

Therefore, there is a need for a selection model that can achieve high accuracy while remaining understandable to coaches.

放在缺口段落最後，明確收束，下一段就可以開始寫研究目的。

GAP-07

指出評估方式缺乏實用性

Most studies report only [[metric]], which offers little guidance on [[practical question]].

Most studies report only overall accuracy, which offers little guidance on which factors coaches should actually monitor.

機器學習論文常見缺口：模型分數高，但使用者不知道怎麼用。很適合帶出 SHAP 等可解釋方法。

GAP-08

指出缺乏外部驗證

In addition, few studies have validated [[model or method]] on [[independent data]].

In addition, few studies have validated their selection models on players from other clubs or regions.

用 In addition 疊加第二個缺口，不必用 First、Second 逐條列，讀起來比較像期刊而不像學位論文。
