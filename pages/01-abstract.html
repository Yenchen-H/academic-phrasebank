ABS

摘要

Abstract

用一段話交代整篇論文：為什麼做、怎麼做、發現什麼、代表什麼。

寫作提醒

  • 常見順序：背景 1 到 2 句 → 缺口 1 句 → 目的 1 句 → 方法 1 到 2 句 → 結果 2 句 → 結論 1 句，全文約 200 到 250 字。
  • 結果一定要放具體數字（準確率、權重、相關係數），只寫 performed well 等於沒寫。
  • 時態：背景與結論用現在式，目的、方法、結果用過去式。
  • 結論要和結果的強度相符，AUC 只有 0.65 就不要寫 highly accurate。

段落結構

  1. 交代背景與問題ABS-01
  2. 點出缺口ABS-02
  3. 說明研究目的ABS-03
  4. 簡述資料與方法ABS-04
  5. 報告主要結果ABS-05ABS-06
  6. 給出結論與意涵ABS-07
ABS-01

一句交代背景與問題

[[Topic]] is essential for [[goal]], but [[problem]].

Early identification of talented players is essential for youth development programs, but current selection still relies heavily on subjective judgment.

摘要第一句同時放重要性與問題，比分成兩句更精簡。but 後面就是全文要解決的事。

ABS-02

點出缺口並銜接目的

[[Approach]] offers [[capability]], yet its application to [[context]] remains limited.

Machine learning offers a data-driven way to support selection, yet its application to youth football remains limited.

先講新方法的潛力，再用 yet 轉到缺口，下一句就可以直接接 This study aimed to。

ABS-03

說明研究目的

This study aimed to [[goal]] using [[method]].

This study aimed to develop an interpretable selection model for youth football players using random forest and SHAP.

摘要的目的句通常用過去式 aimed to，和正文 Introduction 的現在式 aims to 不同。

ABS-04

簡述資料與方法

Data from [[number]] [[participants]] were collected, and [[method]] was used to [[purpose]].

Data from 120 players were collected, and the fuzzy AHP was used to weight 19 selection indicators.

一句交代樣本數和核心方法即可，細節留在正文。

ABS-05

報告主要量化結果

[[Model]] achieved [[metric]] of [[value]], outperforming [[baseline]].

The proposed model achieved an F1-score of 0.84, outperforming logistic regression and support vector machine.

摘要中最重要的一句，一定要有數字和比較對象。

ABS-06

報告解釋性發現

[[Analysis]] revealed that [[features]] were the most influential predictors of [[outcome]].

SHAP analysis revealed that decision making and ball control were the most influential predictors of selection.

有做 SHAP 或權重分析時，用這句交代「哪些因素最重要」，讀者最想知道這個。

ABS-07

給出結論與意涵

These findings suggest that [[X]] can serve as [[role]] for [[users]].

These findings suggest that the proposed model can serve as a transparent reference for coaches during player selection.

用 suggest 和 can 保留語氣，並點出誰會受益。
