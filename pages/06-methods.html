MTD

研究方法

Methods

清楚描述資料、流程與參數，讓其他研究者可以照著重做。

寫作提醒

  • 時態：自己做過的步驟用過去式（were collected, was applied），方法本身的定義與公式用現在式。
  • 常見小節順序：研究流程 → 參與者與倫理 → 資料蒐集 → 分析方法 → 評估與統計。
  • 每個方法選擇都要簡短交代理由，沒做的處理（例如沒用 SMOTE）也要說明為什麼。
  • 參數、樣本數、軟體版本、顯著水準要寫清楚，這是審稿人最常要求補充的地方。

MTD-01

介紹整體研究流程

The overall framework of this study is shown in [[Figure X]] and consists of [[number]] stages.

The overall framework of this study is shown in Figure 1 and consists of three stages.

方法章節第一句，先給讀者全貌，再逐步展開。

MTD-02

描述資料來源與樣本

Data were collected from [[number]] [[participants]] who [[inclusion criteria]].

Data were collected from 120 youth football players who had at least two years of competitive experience.

data 在正式寫作常當複數，所以用 were。收案條件用 who 子句交代。

MTD-03

說明專家問卷的設計

A questionnaire was designed based on [[source]] and reviewed by [[number]] experts in [[field]].

A questionnaire was designed based on the literature review and reviewed by five experts in football coaching.

MCDM 研究常用，說明問卷的效度來源。專家人數太少容易被質疑，可補一句選擇專家的標準。

MTD-04

說明使用某方法的理由

[[Method]] was chosen because it [[reason]].

Random forest was chosen because it handles nonlinear relationships well and provides feature importance scores.

審稿人常問 why this method，簡短交代理由就能避免被追問。

MTD-05

描述參數設定

The model was trained with [[parameter A]] set to [[value]] and [[parameter B]] set to [[value]].

The model was trained with the number of trees set to 500 and the maximum depth set to 10.

參數多時可以改成表格，句子只寫 The main hyperparameters are listed in Table 2.

MTD-06

說明評估指標

Model performance was evaluated using [[metric A]] and [[metric B]].

Model performance was evaluated using accuracy and the F1-score.

指標若不常見，下一句補上公式或簡短定義。

MTD-07

交代倫理審查與同意書

The study was approved by [[ethics committee]], and all participants provided written informed consent.

The study was approved by the university research ethics committee, and all participants provided written informed consent.

有人體受試者的研究必備。未成年受試者要改成 their parents or guardians provided consent。

MTD-08

說明依循的研究準則

This study was conducted and reported in accordance with [[guideline]].

This study was conducted and reported in accordance with the TRIPOD guideline for prediction models.

預測模型、系統性回顧、臨床研究都有對應的報告準則，寫出來能增加可信度。

MTD-09

說明一致性檢定的處理

Pairwise comparison matrices with a consistency ratio above [[threshold]] were returned to the experts for revision.

Pairwise comparison matrices with a consistency ratio above 0.1 were returned to the experts for revision.

AHP 研究不只要報告 CR，也要說明不通過時怎麼處理，這是常被漏掉的細節。

MTD-10

說明交叉驗證方式

[[k]]-fold stratified cross-validation was used to evaluate [[model]] and reduce the effect of [[issue]].

Ten-fold stratified cross-validation was used to evaluate the model and reduce the effect of the small sample size.

stratified 表示每一折保留相同的類別比例，類別不平衡時一定要寫。

MTD-11

說明沒有採用某處理的理由

No [[technique]] was applied because [[reason]].

No oversampling technique was applied because the two classes were reasonably balanced.

主動說明「沒做什麼」可以先回答審稿人的疑問，避免被要求補做。

MTD-12

說明統計顯著水準

All statistical analyses were performed using [[software]], and the significance level was set at [[alpha]].

All statistical analyses were performed using Python 3.11, and the significance level was set at 0.05.

放在統計分析小節最後，一句交代軟體與顯著水準。
