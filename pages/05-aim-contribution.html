AIM

研究目的與貢獻

Aim & Contribution

說明本研究要做什麼、怎麼做，以及跟前人比起來多了什麼。

寫作提醒

  • 目的句緊接在研究缺口之後，讓讀者看出「因為缺這個，所以我做這個」。
  • 貢獻可以條列 2 到 3 點，但每點要具體，不要只寫 propose a novel framework。
  • 時態：Introduction 中的目的用現在式（aims to），摘要與討論中回顧目的用過去式（aimed to）。
  • Introduction 最後一段可以簡單交代全文架構。

段落結構

  2. 提出研究問題或假設AIM-07AIM-08
  3. 說明貢獻AIM-03AIM-04AIM-05

AIM-01

說明本研究的目的

This study aims to [[goal]] by [[approach]].

This study aims to identify key selection indicators for youth football players by combining the fuzzy AHP with random forest.

aims to 加 by 一句話同時交代目的與方法，是最常用的寫法。

AIM-02

回應前面的研究缺口

To address this gap, we propose [[method]] that [[key feature]].

To address this gap, we propose a hybrid framework that integrates expert weights with data-driven feature importance.

To address this gap 直接呼應上一段，結構清楚，審稿人很好讀。

AIM-03

列出主要貢獻

The main contributions of this study are as follows.

The main contributions of this study are as follows.

後面接條列，每一點用完整句子，例如 First, we construct... Second, we show...

AIM-04

說明方法上的貢獻

Unlike previous studies that [[limitation]], the proposed method [[advantage]].

Unlike previous studies that treat all indicators equally, the proposed method assigns weights based on both expert opinion and model output.

用 Unlike 直接對比前人，讓貢獻一目了然。

AIM-05

說明實務上的貢獻

The results can provide [[users]] with [[practical value]] when [[situation]].

The results can provide coaches with a transparent reference when selecting players for regional training teams.

SCI 期刊常要求說明實務價值，這句放在貢獻最後一點很適合。

AIM-06

介紹全文架構

The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. Section 2 reviews [[topic]], and Section 3 describes [[topic]].

The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. Section 2 reviews related work on talent identification, and Section 3 describes the proposed framework.

Introduction 的最後一段，可依章節數往下延伸，用逗號或分開成兩句即可，不要用分號串接。

AIM-07

提出研究問題

Specifically, this study addresses two questions: (1) [[question 1]] and (2) [[question 2]].

Specifically, this study addresses two questions: (1) which indicators matter most to coaches and (2) whether a model can reproduce their decisions.

目的比較抽象時，用研究問題把它具體化，後面的結果章節也可以依問題順序回答。

AIM-08

提出研究假設

It was hypothesized that [[hypothesis]].

It was hypothesized that the markerless system would show high agreement with the force plate.

驗證型研究（新工具和黃金標準比較）常寫假設。注意這裡用 would，因為是從研究開始前的角度描述。

AIM-09

說明比較型研究目的

The aim of this study is to determine whether [[outcome]] differs between [[group A]], [[group B]], and [[group C]].

The aim of this study is to determine whether indicator weights differ between coaches, scouts, and sport scientists.

比較不同組別、賽季或條件的研究最適合。determine whether 表示答案可能是有差異也可能沒有，比 prove that 中立。
