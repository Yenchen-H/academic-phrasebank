RES

結果呈現

Results

客觀呈現研究發現，引導讀者看圖表的重點，先不做過多解釋。

寫作提醒

  • 結果章節以描述為主，少用 may、might，解釋原因留到討論章節。
  • 有統計檢定時，p 值和效果量要一起報告。顯著但效果量很小時要直接說明，見「統計分析與報告」頁。
  • 不要只寫 as shown in Table 3，要說出表格中最重要的發現。
  • 時態：發現用過去式，指涉圖表用現在式（Table 3 shows）。
  • 同一個數值在全文要一致，結果、討論、結論出現的數字要互相核對。

段落結構

  1. 呈現主要結果並指引圖表RES-01RES-02
  2. 比較模型或組別RES-03RES-04RES-07
  3. 呈現解釋性分析RES-05RES-08
  4. 報告穩健性檢驗RES-06RES-09RES-10
RES-01

引導讀者看圖表

As shown in [[Table X]], [[main finding]].

As shown in Table 3, technical skills received the highest weight among the five dimensions.

As shown in 後面一定要接發現本身，而不是只說「結果如表所示」。

RES-02

呈現排序結果

The top three [[items]] were [[A]], [[B]], and [[C]], which together accounted for [[percentage]] of [[total]].

The top three indicators were ball control, decision making, and sprint speed, which together accounted for 48% of the total weight.

AHP、TOPSIS 等排序型研究很常用，最後補上累積比例更有說服力。

RES-03

比較不同模型表現

[[Model A]] outperformed [[Model B]] in terms of [[metric]].

The proposed model outperformed the baseline logistic regression in terms of both accuracy and recall.

outperformed 後面記得用 in terms of 說明比較的面向。

RES-04

描述沒有顯著差異的結果

No significant difference was found between [[group A]] and [[group B]] in [[variable]].

No significant difference was found between forwards and midfielders in aerobic capacity.

不顯著的結果也要寫，避免只報好消息，用被動語態較中性。

RES-05

說明 SHAP 等解釋結果

The SHAP analysis indicated that [[feature]] had the largest impact on [[prediction]].

The SHAP analysis indicated that decision making had the largest impact on the predicted selection outcome.

indicated 比 proved 保守，結果章節避免用 prove。

RES-06

呈現一致性檢定結果

All consistency ratios were below [[threshold]], indicating that [[conclusion]].

All consistency ratios were below 0.1, indicating that the expert judgments were acceptable.

AHP 研究必備的一句，交代問卷結果可信。

RES-07

報告平均值與標準差

[[Metric]] was [[mean ± SD]] across the [[k]] folds.

The recall of the proposed model was 0.82 ± 0.05 across the ten folds.

交叉驗證的結果要附標準差，讀者才能判斷模型穩不穩定。

RES-08

說明特徵影響方向

Higher values of [[feature]] were associated with positive SHAP values, indicating a higher likelihood of [[outcome]].

Higher values of decision-making scores were associated with positive SHAP values, indicating a higher likelihood of selection.

除了「哪個特徵最重要」，也要交代「方向」，也就是數值越高越有利還是越不利。

RES-09

報告敏感度分析

The sensitivity analysis showed that the ranking of [[alternatives]] remained stable when [[weights]] were varied by [[range]].

The sensitivity analysis showed that the ranking of the top five players remained stable when the criteria weights were varied by ±20%.

MCDM 研究的審稿人幾乎都會要求敏感度分析，這句用來交代排序結果不是偶然。

RES-10

比較不同方法的排序一致性

The rankings obtained by [[method A]] and [[method B]] were highly consistent ([[statistic]]).

The rankings obtained by TOPSIS and VIKOR were highly consistent (Spearman's ρ = 0.91).

多種 MCDM 方法交叉比對時使用，括號內放相關係數，簡潔又有證據。
