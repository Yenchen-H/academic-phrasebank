結果呈現

Results

客觀呈現研究發現，引導讀者看圖表的重點，先不做過多解釋。

寫作提醒

  • 結果章節以描述為主，解釋原因留到討論章節。
  • 不要只寫 as shown in Table 3，要說出表格中最重要的發現。
  • 數字和圖表要一致，同一個數值只在一處詳細呈現。
RES-01

引導讀者看圖表

As shown in [[Table X]], [[main finding]].

As shown in Table 3, technical skills received the highest weight among the five dimensions.

As shown in 後面一定要接發現本身，而不是只說「結果如表所示」。

RES-02

呈現排序結果

The top three [[items]] were [[A]], [[B]], and [[C]], which together accounted for [[percentage]] of [[total]].

The top three indicators were ball control, decision making, and sprint speed, which together accounted for 48% of the total weight.

AHP、TOPSIS 等排序型研究很常用，最後補上累積比例更有說服力。

RES-03

比較不同模型表現

[[Model A]] outperformed [[Model B]] in terms of [[metric]].

The proposed model outperformed the baseline logistic regression in terms of both accuracy and recall.

outperformed 後面記得用 in terms of 說明比較的面向。

RES-04

描述沒有顯著差異的結果

No significant difference was found between [[group A]] and [[group B]] in [[variable]].

No significant difference was found between forwards and midfielders in aerobic capacity.

不顯著的結果也要寫，避免只報好消息，用被動語態較中性。

RES-05

說明 SHAP 等解釋結果

The SHAP analysis indicated that [[feature]] had the largest impact on [[prediction]].

The SHAP analysis indicated that decision making had the largest impact on the predicted selection outcome.

indicated 比 proved 保守，結果章節避免用 prove。

RES-06

呈現一致性檢定結果

All consistency ratios were below [[threshold]], indicating that [[conclusion]].

All consistency ratios were below 0.1, indicating that the expert judgments were acceptable.

AHP 研究必備的一句，交代問卷結果可信。
