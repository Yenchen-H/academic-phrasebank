STA

統計分析與報告

Statistics Reporting

交代統計檢定的前提、方法與效果量，讓結果經得起審稿人檢查。

寫作提醒

  • 順序：先檢查前提（常態、變異數同質）→ 依前提與組數選擇檢定 → 定義效果量標準 → 報告結果。
  • 時態：方法與結果都用過去式（was assessed, was used, showed）。
  • p 值一定要搭配效果量一起報告，只有 p < .05 不代表差異有實際意義。
  • 數字格式全文統一，例如 p = .02、d = 0.21，括號內用逗號分隔，不要用分號。

段落結構

  1. 檢查檢定前提STA-01
  2. 說明檢定方法的選擇STA-02STA-03
  3. 定義效果量標準STA-04
  4. 報告檢定結果STA-05
STA-01

交代檢定前提的檢查

[[Assumption A]] was assessed using [[test A]], and [[assumption B]] using [[test B]].

Normality of the jump height data was assessed using the Shapiro-Wilk test, and homogeneity of variance using Levene's test.

統計分析小節的第一句。後半句省略 was assessed，避免同一個動詞重複兩次。

STA-02

說明改用無母數檢定的理由

Since the assumptions of [[A]] and [[B]] were not met, [[test]] was used to [[comparison]].

Since the assumptions of normality and equal variance were not met, the Kruskal-Wallis test was used to compare the three age groups.

緊接在前提檢查之後，用 Since 交代因果，審稿人就不會問為什麼不用 ANOVA。

STA-03

依組數說明檢定方法

[[Test A]] was used to compare two independent groups, and [[test B]] was used when three or more groups were compared.

The Mann-Whitney U test was used to compare two playing positions, and the Kruskal-Wallis test was used when three or more positions were compared.

一句交代兩種情境各用什麼檢定，比分成兩個獨立句子更緊湊。

STA-04

定義效果量判斷標準

Effect size was assessed using [[index]] and interpreted as [[level 1]] ([[value]]), [[level 2]] ([[value]]), or [[level 3]] ([[value]]) [[citation]].

Effect size was assessed using Cohen's d and interpreted as small (0.2), medium (0.5), or large (0.8) [25].

先定義標準，結果章節才能說 small 或 large。範圍值用 to 連接（例如 0.01 to 0.06），不要用連接號。

STA-05

報告顯著但效果量小

Although [[group A]] showed higher [[variable]] than [[group B]] ([[statistics]]), the effect size was small.

Although forwards showed higher sprint speed than defenders (p = .02, d = 0.21), the effect size was small.

樣本數大時很容易顯著，這句誠實交代差異雖然顯著但實際影響有限，討論章節可再解釋其意義。
