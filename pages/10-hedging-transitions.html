HDG

語氣保留與轉折

Hedging & Transitions

跨章節通用的句型，讓推論不過度宣稱，段落之間銜接自然。

寫作提醒

  • 語氣強弱要看章節：結果章節少用 hedge，討論章節多用，結論再稍微收緊。
  • 常用的保留字：may、might、could、suggest、indicate、appear to、tend to、likely、possibly。
  • 避免過度宣稱的字：prove、clearly demonstrate、always、perfectly。
  • 轉折詞優先用 However、Nevertheless、In addition，避免每段都用 Moreover、Furthermore 開頭。

HDG-01

保守地提出解釋

This difference may be partly explained by [[factor]].

This difference may be partly explained by the younger age of our participants.

may be partly explained 同時保留兩層語氣：可能是、而且只解釋一部分。

HDG-02

推論但說明需要更多證據

These results may indicate that [[interpretation]], although further evidence is needed.

These results may indicate that cognitive skills develop earlier than physical skills, although further evidence is needed.

推論較大膽時，句尾補上 although further evidence is needed，審稿人比較能接受。

HDG-03

描述看起來有關聯

[[A]] appears to be related to [[B]] in [[context]].

Sprint performance appears to be related to selection outcome in younger age groups.

appears to be 用在相關但未證實因果的情況，比 is related to 保守。

HDG-04

提醒讀者解讀要謹慎

Nevertheless, [[limitation]] should be considered when interpreting these results.

Nevertheless, the small number of experts should be considered when interpreting these results.

從正面結果轉到限制的常用轉折句，Nevertheless 比 But 正式。

HDG-05

承接上一段的發現

Building on these findings, [[next step]].

Building on these findings, the next stage examined whether the weights could improve model accuracy.

段落開頭用來表示「接著往下做」，讓方法或結果的流程更連貫。

HDG-06

補充另一個面向

In addition to [[A]], [[B]] also [[effect]].

In addition to technical skills, decision making also contributed strongly to the final ranking.

比 Moreover 開頭更具體，因為句子本身就說明了補充的是什麼。

HDG-07

描述有趨勢但差異不大

[[Group A]] tended to [[trend]] compared with [[group B]], but the difference was small.

Early-born players tended to receive higher scores compared with late-born players, but the difference was small.

tended to 搭配 the difference was small，忠實呈現「有傾向但不明顯」的結果。

HDG-08

界定研究的重點

Rather than [[alternative goal]], this study focused on [[actual goal]].

Rather than maximizing predictive accuracy, this study focused on producing results that coaches can interpret.

預先回應「為什麼準確率不是最高」這類質疑，清楚劃定研究的範圍。
