CON

結論

Conclusion

簡短回顧研究做了什麼、發現什麼、有什麼貢獻，為全文收尾。

寫作提醒

  • 結論通常 1 到 2 段，不放新的數據、圖表或引用。
  • 結論的語氣要和結果強度相符，模型表現普通就不要寫 highly effective。
  • 時態：回顧做過的事用過去式，陳述貢獻與意涵用現在式，未來工作用 will 或 could。

段落結構

  1. 重述研究內容CON-01
  2. 總結主要發現CON-02
  3. 說明貢獻與定位CON-03CON-04
  4. 限制與展望CON-05CON-06
CON-01

重述研究內容

This study proposed [[method]] to [[goal]].

This study proposed a hybrid selection framework to support coaches in identifying talented youth players.

結論第一句，用一句話回顧整個研究，不要再重複背景。

CON-02

總結主要發現

The results showed that [[main finding]].

The results showed that combining expert weights with model-based importance produced more stable rankings than either approach alone.

只挑最重要的 1 到 2 個發現，細節數字留在結果章節。

CON-03

定位研究的角色

[[X]] should be seen as a complement to, rather than a replacement for, [[Y]].

The proposed model should be seen as a complement to, rather than a replacement for, coaches' experience.

人機協作類研究常用的收尾，語氣中肯，不會過度宣稱。

CON-04

說明研究的累積貢獻

This study adds to the growing evidence that [[claim]].

This study adds to the growing evidence that markerless pose estimation can be used for field-based movement assessment.

把自己的研究放回整個領域的脈絡，表示研究是在累積知識。

CON-05

在限制下肯定價值

Although [[limitation]], the proposed approach provides [[value]].

Although the sample was limited to one region, the proposed approach provides a practical starting point for transparent player selection.

結論中提限制要簡短，用 Although 帶過再回到價值。

CON-06

說明未來工作

Future work will extend [[method]] to [[new context]].

Future work will extend the framework to other team sports and to female players.

結論的最後一句，用 will 表示作者自己的後續計畫；若是建議其他研究者，改用 could。
