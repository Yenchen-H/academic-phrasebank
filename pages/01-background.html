BG

研究背景

Background

交代研究領域的重要性與現況，讓讀者知道為什麼這個題目值得被研究。

寫作提醒

  • 由大到小：領域重要性 → 目前常用做法 → 做法的侷限，最後一句接到研究缺口。
  • 每段只講一件事，段落短一點，引用不要一句塞三篇以上。
  • 現況描述用現在完成式（have been widely used），既定事實用現在式。
BG-01

說明研究領域的重要性

[[Topic]] plays a key role in [[field or outcome]].

Talent identification plays a key role in the long-term development of competitive sport.

背景第一句最常見的開法，plays a key role 比 is very important 正式，也比 is of paramount importance 自然。

BG-02

描述某方法已被廣泛使用

[[Method]] has been widely used to [[purpose]] in [[context]].

The analytic hierarchy process has been widely used to rank evaluation criteria in sports management.

用現在完成式表示「到目前為止一直都是這樣」，後面通常接 2 到 3 篇代表性文獻。

BG-03

指出近年的研究趨勢

In recent years, there has been growing interest in [[topic]], particularly in [[sub-area]].

In recent years, there has been growing interest in markerless pose estimation, particularly in field-based sports settings.

growing interest 用來帶出熱門程度，particularly in 可以順便把範圍縮小到你的題目。

BG-04

說明實務上的需求

In practice, [[stakeholders]] often need to [[task]] under [[constraint]].

In practice, coaches often need to select athletes under limited time and incomplete performance data.

從使用者或現場角度切入，讓讀者感受到問題是真的存在，不只是學術上的好奇。

BG-05

介紹技術的發展帶來新可能

Advances in [[technology]] have made it possible to [[new capability]].

Advances in deep learning have made it possible to estimate joint positions from a single video camera.

常用在背景的中段，作為從傳統方法轉向新方法的橋梁。

BG-06

歸納既有研究的主要方向

Existing studies on [[topic]] can be broadly divided into two groups: [[group A]] and [[group B]].

Existing studies on athlete selection can be broadly divided into two groups: expert-based evaluation and data-driven prediction.

幫讀者整理文獻地圖，接下來可以各用一段介紹兩類研究，再指出兩者的不足。
